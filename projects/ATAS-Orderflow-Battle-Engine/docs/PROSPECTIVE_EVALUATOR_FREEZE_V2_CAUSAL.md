# PROSPECTIVE EVALUATOR FREEZE V2 — CAUSAL

Frozen: 2026-08-25 after the 30-day ResearchDataBattle outcome sample was exposed.
Scope: NQ/MNQ order-flow research only; manual decision support; no order execution.

## Why V2 exists

V1 and all rules evaluated against the now-exposed 30-day sample are historical research artifacts. The 30-day sample contains 8,234 bars, 29 complete sessions and 147 candidates and showed that the earlier 63.6% result did not generalize. Therefore no result from that sample may be called prospective validation of V2.

V2 starts a new evidence window strictly after this freeze.

## Frozen research lanes

### Lane A — General causal engine
Purpose: test whether temporal causal ordering + evidence independence + ablation robustness improves the original general candidate engine.

Compare four nested subsets:
1. original candidate
2. temporal-order-valid causal candidate
3. causal + >=3 independent evidence families + real response
4. causal + independence + ablation-robust candidate

No thresholds may be changed based on the exposed 30-day outcomes.

Primary horizon: 20 M5 bars / 100 minutes.
Secondary: 5, 10, 40 bars.

### Lane B — SELL-only long-horizon hypothesis
Defined separately in `SELL_LONG_HORIZON_FREEZE_V1.md`.
Primary horizon: 40 M5 bars / 200 minutes.
This is a new hypothesis generated by the exposed data and must use only post-freeze outcomes.

## Common event semantics

- decision exists after completed closed M5 bar
- entry reference is next consecutive M5 open
- 0.25 point tick
- costs: 4 ticks primary, 8 ticks stress
- any session change, gap or timestamp discontinuity censors the event
- fixed outcome-blind cooldown per lane
- BUY and SELL always reported separately in Lane A
- Wilson 95% hit-rate interval
- session/block bootstrap for expectancy
- matched controls required

## Controls

Lane A controls:
- matched 3-bar price direction
- original non-causal candidate

Lane B controls:
- matched 3-bar price direction
- always-SELL at identical timestamps
- original non-causal SELL candidate

## General causal promotion-to-next-stage gates

Necessary, not sufficient:
- >=100 causal-order-valid events total
- >=30 BUY and >=30 SELL
- >=20 complete sessions
- primary 4-tick PF >=1.25
- primary mean expectancy >0 for both sides
- stress expectancy >=0 for both sides
- session-bootstrap lower bound >=0 overall and no catastrophic side-specific lower bound
- top five positive events <=50% of positive ticks
- best positive session <=30% of positive ticks
- first-half and second-half expectancy same sign
- causal subsets must outperform original candidate or materially improve MAE/drawdown while retaining expectancy
- causal + ablation-robust subset must not depend on one evidence family

## Anti-overfitting

- exposed 30-day outcomes may not tune V2 thresholds
- do not disable BUY in Lane A because BUY failed in the exposed sample
- do not alter the 40-bar SELL horizon after seeing new SELL outcomes
- do not select a new control after results arrive
- do not loosen cooldown after seeing clusters
- any semantic/threshold/horizon/control change creates V3 and restarts the evidence clock

## Output required for every evaluation

- exact data start/end and fingerprint
- event counts by side/session/regime
- hit rate + Wilson interval
- expectancy and PF at 4/8 ticks
- MFE/MAE distributions
- concentration metrics
- first-half/second-half split
- session bootstrap
- control increment
- causal-order failures
- evidence-family concentration
- ablation results
- reason-code distribution

## Interpretation

Passing V2 does not authorize automated trading. It authorizes realistic execution simulation, calibration, parity checks and an additional untouched paper-forward stage.
